# Changelog

## 0.7.0

- Contract 0.7.0: `extractWidget` and `convertToStatefulWidget`.

## 0.6.0

- Contract 0.6.0: `getRuntimeTree`, `RuntimeNode`, `Bounds` and
  `SceneElement.className`.

## 0.5.0

- Contract 0.5.0: `AgentInfo.isWidgetCreationTracked`.

## 0.4.0

- Contract 0.4.0: the extensions of the design agent, `hello` and `scene`,
  and the types `AgentInfo`, `Scene` and `SceneElement`.
- The package accepts Dart 3.10, the SDK of the oldest supported Flutter.

## 0.3.0

- Contract 0.3.0: `getSourceTree` and the type `SourceNode`.

## 0.2.0

- Contract 0.2.0: `getStructureOptions`, `wrapWidget`, `removeWidget`,
  `reorderWidget` and `swapWidget`.

## 0.1.0

- Contract 0.1.0: `getVersion`, `getSelection`, `select`, `getProperties`,
  `setProperty`, `undo` and `redo`; events `selectionChanged` and
  `documentChanged`.
